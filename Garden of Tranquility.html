Panic Attacks

When Millie was still around, I was extremely unmedicated for anxiety.

I would have nightly panic attacks over nothing, or something miniscule. Either one of the two.

She would climb up onto my bed (surprisingly well for a blind senior cat) and lay near my head.

I'd lay my head onto her front paws as she purred as loud as she possibly could. She stayed until I stopped crying.

You know how cats have a healing frequency in their purr? I fully believe she healed me everytime I cried. Everytime she purred.

I miss that greatly. Now, when I have panic attacks, I can't do much but wait it out. I wish she were still here.

On the plus side, I've had less and less. I still get sad from time to time, but, it's life. You're bound to get sad.

Sweetheart

She was the sweetest girl ever. Purred constantly. Squeaked anytime I said her name. She loved her papa.

Only time she got mean was because I pestered her when she was a bit younger. She was around 15-16. She scratched my arm up.

I still have her scars on my right arm. I hope they never heal.
