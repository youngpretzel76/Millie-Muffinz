Constant

The pain has subsided a bit. It's not as overwhelming all at once. There are times where it still hurts to breathe or even think about it, but, I'm better.

I think she'd want me to be better, anyway. All that panic attack care and I rarely got better.

I just still hope I made that right choice. People have reassured me, but it still lingers. It probably will for a while. I'll come to terms with it eventually.

I know she's happy across the rainbow bridge now. I hope she can run around. I don't know what she was like as a kitten, so hopefully if she was healthier then, I hope she took that form.

One day, I'll see her again. I'll see all of them again.

Help

I struggled to ask for help for the longest time. I refused to open up about my struggles until I was actively broken and could not contain it myself. I really only asked my girlfriend.

I've learned to accept help from people. I'm trying to do better, not just in the mental aspect, but overall.

I wish Millie's death wasn't the ultimate catalyst that caused me to get my shit together, but honestly, I don't think I was going to be able to had she still been here.

Not that I didn't want to, but my room was DISGUSTING. Taking care of a very old senior cat is not easy. That paired with depression and executive dysfunction? I was a mess.

My dad redid my room, so now it's assembled differently. More cleaner. There's still a long way to go, but it's way better.

I've been channeling my grief into things that would benefit me. I've been going to the gym, eating better, studying more.

I just hope Millie knows how much I love her, how much I miss her, and how much she has helped me. I mean, hell, she's still helping me NOW.

Thank you, Millie. I'll always remember you. I'll always love you. Love, Papa.
